<ASSET-THESIS — TUNGSTEN LEADS>
Tungsten is the lead asset for one reason: its moat is physics, and nobody can print more of it.

- **No substitute.** Highest melting point of any metal (3,422°C). Hardest metal. Gold-adjacent density. Armor-piercing penetrators, chip-fab (WF6 gas), cutting tools, fusion research — no replacement exists in the applications that matter most.
- **Verified price action.** European APT benchmark +557% since China's February 2025 export controls ($340 → ~$2,250/MTU, Mar 2026). Carbide powder +600%+ since Jan 2025; some benchmarks ~9x in two years. Concentrate roughly 6x ($190-210 → $1,200-1,400/MTU).
- **Structural, not cyclical.** China controls ~80% of global output. Export licensing with end-use declarations (prove it's not going into munitions). Mining quotas cut three years running (~14% cumulative). Inventories near three-year lows. The clearing mechanism is a bureaucrat's stamp in Beijing — scarcity administered by paperwork.
- **The incentive trap.** While the Hormuz energy choke is live, Beijing has zero reason to relieve the second chokepoint. Leverage > export revenue. They sell to everyone else at scarcity prices; the US gets the paperwork diet.
- **The catalyst.** Washington's answer to a stacked chokepoint is money: Defense Production Act grants, low-rate loans, guaranteed offtake. The Pentagon will be sourcing tungsten — that's the bet. Every ex-China junior with permitted pounds in the ground holds an unpriced call option that converts to a funded national-security asset.
- **Trade line:** China holds the leverage, so America buys the rocks. The chokepoint is the pitch; the DPA is the catalyst; the juniors are the vehicle.
</ASSET-THESIS>
